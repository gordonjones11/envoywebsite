We hire people who have run the equipment.

Envoy turns industrial data into guidance mills can act on. That work only holds up when the person behind it has stood in front of the machine, which is why nearly everyone here came from a mill before they came to us.

Working Here

What the job actually looks like.

Remote, and genuinely so

The team is distributed and has been since long before it was common. The work is collaborative, but it does not depend on everyone being in one building.

Mill experience is the qualification

We are not looking for people to learn pulp and paper on the job. We are looking for people who already know what a wet end does at 3 a.m. and can explain it to somebody who doesn't.

Your judgment is the product

Every report goes out with an engineer's name behind it. That is the job: reading what the analysis found and deciding what it actually means for this mill, this week.

Engineers and developers in the same room

Our software team sits alongside the engineers rather than apart from them. If a tool is getting in your way, the person who can change it is on the call.
