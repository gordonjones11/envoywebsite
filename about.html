Company:   About Envoy

Real engineers. Real insights. Real results.

Envoy is a group of process engineers with real mill experience who turn operational data into guidance a team can act on, delivered before the shift ends. We work with pulp and paper mills to cut downtime, improve process reliability, and be there when the answer isn't obvious.

Our Story

Founded in 2003, by a mill engineer.

Envoy started when a former mill engineer and software professional set up a consulting practice for mills working with advanced control technology. The work began on site, one mill at a time.

What those visits kept showing was that the hard part wasn't the technology. It was that nobody had time to look at the data every day, and the engineer who could interpret it wasn't there when it mattered. So the on-site consulting became a remote support model: daily engineer-reviewed reports, and deeper analysis every month, for mills that needed the judgment more often than they needed the visit.

Twenty-three years later that is still the shape of the business. The tools have changed a great deal. The premise has not.

What We Believe

Four things we keep coming back to.

Insight only matters if it leads to action

A finding nobody owns is the same as no finding at all. Everything we deliver is built to be acted on by a specific person, on a specific shift, this week.

Real experience beats automation alone

Software finds the pattern. An engineer who has run a machine knows whether it means anything. Every report we send has been read by one of ours before it reaches you.

You shouldn't need a long contract to get long-term value

We would rather earn the next year than lock in the next three. Our clients stay because the work is worth it, which is why 95% of them renew.

We want to be the team you call when the answer isn't obvious

Anyone can tell you what happened. The value is in the harder question underneath it, and that's the call we want to be on.

The Team

Twenty engineers who have actually run the equipment.
